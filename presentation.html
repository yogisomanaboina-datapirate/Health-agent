Deep Dive

Emergency Triage & ICU Bed Allocation

Triage Acuity Algorithm
  • 1. Three-Tier Acuity Scoring: Classifies condition into EMERGENT, URGENT, or NON_URGENT.
  • 2. Vital Sign Validation: Evaluates HR (>100 bpm), BP (>140/90), and SpO2 (<94%) alongside narrative symptoms.
  • 3. Red-Flag Escalation: Detects acute coronary syndromes, stroke (FAST criteria), and pulmonary distress instantly.
Haversine GPS & Bed Booking
  • 1. GPS Haversine Geometry: Calculates true straight-line and road distances from user coordinates to hospitals.
  • 2. Live Inventory Deduction: Real-time deduction of 1 ICU bed from database upon mission confirmation.
  • 3. ALS Telemetry: Generates unique Mission ID, ETA, paramedic crew roster, and emergency hospital alert.
